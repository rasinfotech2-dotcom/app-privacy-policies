1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how NDroidHub ("we", "our", or "us") manages information when you install and use Status & Reels Saver Pro (referred to as the "App"). We respect your fundamental right to privacy and have engineered our app using zero-personal-data architecture.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do not collect any personal identifying information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide your name, phone number, or email to use any feature.
  • No Cloud Media Storage: Any status photos or videos viewed, saved, or sliced remain entirely in your device's local storage and MediaStore. We have no servers capable of accessing your media.
  • No Contact Harvesting: The Direct Chat feature allows you to message phone numbers via WhatsApp without saving them. Phone numbers entered in this tool are used solely to launch the official WhatsApp application intent and are never sent over the internet or saved to remote databases.

3. Android Permissions & Storage Access Framework (SAF)

To deliver its core features while complying strictly with Google Play Store Scoped Storage policies on Android 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15, the App uses the following sanctioned APIs:

  • Storage Access Framework (ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT_TREE): The App asks you to select the specific WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business status directory. This permission gives the App access ONLY to the user-selected folder and does not give broad access to your entire storage drive. We strictly avoid high-risk permissions such as MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.
  • Android MediaStore (Scoped Gallery Storage): When you choose to save a photo, video, or audio clip to your phone, the App uses Android's standard MediaStore.Images, MediaStore.Video, and MediaStore.Audio APIs without requiring legacy storage runtime permissions on Android 10+.
  • Package Visibility (<queries>): The App checks whether WhatsApp (com.whatsapp) or WhatsApp Business (com.whatsapp.w4b) is installed on your device to tailor its user interface. We do not use the broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.
  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13+, the App requests permission to deliver offline daily streak and tool utility reminders. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device with zero cloud push servers.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Mediation
