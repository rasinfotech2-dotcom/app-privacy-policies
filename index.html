🔒 Core Commitment: NDroidHub Mobile Applications are entirely on-device utility applications. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or analyze your photos, videos, contacts, or messages. All processing happens 100% locally on your device.

1. Overview & Scope

This Universal Privacy Policy governs all mobile applications, utilities, and digital tools published by NDroidHub ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your fundamental right to privacy and have engineered our mobile applications using a strict Zero-Personal-Data and On-Device Scoped Execution architecture.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Across all our applications, we do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Account Registration: You are never required to create an account, log in, or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use any feature.
  • No Cloud Media Storage: Any media viewed, processed, sliced, or generated remains entirely in your device's local memory and storage. We operate zero cloud servers capable of receiving or storing your media.
  • No Contact Harvesting: Utility features that initiate external intents (such as direct messaging) do so solely through official Android system intents without reading, storing, or transmitting your address book or phone contacts.

3. Android Permissions & Storage Access Framework (SAF)

To deliver utility features while complying strictly with Google Play Scoped Storage policies on Android 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15+, this Application uses sanctioned APIs:

  • Storage Access Framework (ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT_TREE): When folder selection is required, the Application requests user consent through Android's system directory picker. The Application accesses only the explicitly granted folder and never accesses broad storage. We strictly avoid high-risk permissions such as MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.
  • Android MediaStore (Scoped Gallery Storage): When saving media to your device, the Application uses standard Android MediaStore.Images, MediaStore.Video, and MediaStore.Audio APIs without requiring legacy storage runtime permissions on Android 10+.
  • Package Visibility (<queries>): The Application checks whether supported messenger or social tools are installed on your device strictly to tailor user interface options. We do not use the broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.
  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13+, the Application requests permission to deliver offline daily streak and utility reminders. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device with zero cloud push servers.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Mediation Transparency

To keep NDroidHub mobile applications freely accessible worldwide across Google Play and over 19 alternative app stores, applications may display non-intrusive advertisements managed by industry-standard mediation platforms (such as Unity LevelPlay / ironSource):

  • These platforms may process anonymized, non-personal technical telemetry (such as device manufacturer, screen resolution, operating system version, and general network region) strictly for fraud prevention, ad capping, and delivery optimization.
  • Store Review Safe Mode: When active, all ad monetization is silenced to guarantee an uninterrupted review experience.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime via your Android system settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our applications are designed as general-audience utilities and are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has interacted with the Application in a manner of concern, contact us immediately for prompt assistance.

6. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have fundamental rights regarding your data. Because our applications store zero user data on remote servers, no remote personal profile exists to delete or export. You maintain complete control over all data on your phone by clearing app cache or uninstalling the Application.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform policy enhancements or feature updates. Any updates will be reflected inside our In-App viewer and at our permanent HTTPS repository.
